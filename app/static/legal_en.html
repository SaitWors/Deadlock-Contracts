PRIVACY NOTICE · PRE-RELEASE

Data processing

Revision dated August 24, 2026

Data we use

SteamID64 and the derived account ID, public Steam display name and avatar, selected contracts, final statistics for linked matches, verification states, XP, technical audit records and server-side sessions. We do not request your Steam password, payment data, Steam email or private messages.

Purposes

Authentication, daily-board creation, locating the first eligible match after a case is locked, evaluating structured conditions, preventing duplicate rewards, troubleshooting and protecting the service.

Sources and processors

Identity is verified through Steam OpenID. Public name and avatar may be retrieved through the Steam Web API. Final match statistics come from a community-operated Deadlock API. The service runs with the hosting provider selected by the project owner.

Companion telemetry

The Overwolf Companion transmits your own supported GEP signals, Match ID, mode, phase and bounded diagnostic statistics. Client telemetry is provisional and cannot independently complete a contract or grant XP. Diagnostic output redacts roster and Steam identifiers.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained until account deletion or the end of testing unless longer retention is required for security or dispute handling. Account deletion is available in Data & Account. Backups may remain until scheduled rotation completes.

Security and contact

Controls include HTTPS, HttpOnly/Secure/SameSite cookies, CSRF protection, hashed sessions, access control, raw-data minimization, internal PostgreSQL and container isolation. No system can guarantee absolute security. For questions about data processing, message @deadlockts_bot on Telegram.

TEST TERMS · PRE-RELEASE

Testing terms

Revision dated August 24, 2026

Project status

Deadlock Contracts is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with Valve. Deadlock, Steam and related marks belong to their respective owners.

Pre-release service

Features, rules, XP and stored data may change. Delays, incomplete results and temporary outages are possible. XP has no monetary value and is not sold.

Acceptable use

Automated spam, bypassing limits, accessing another person's data, exploiting defects, disrupting the service and publishing live secrets or vulnerabilities before responsible disclosure are prohibited.

Gameplay

Contracts must not be used to grief, intentionally lose or violate Deadlock or Steam rules. Objectives are designed to align with winning, but each player remains responsible for their conduct.

Evidence and decisions

Results depend on a third-party community provider. “No data” means evidence is insufficient; it is not an automatic completion or failure. The server evaluator is the only authority for contract verdicts and XP.

Contact

Send security reports privately to @deadlockts_bot on Telegram. Do not publish a working exploit before a fix.

SUPPORT · WEB v0.10

Support

Updated August 25, 2026

Before contacting us

Refresh the page, check the Companion status in your profile and allow post-match verification to finish. Fetching or verification states do not mean the contract failed.

What to include

Include the time, mode, Match ID if available, website version and Companion version. Never send Steam passwords, session cookies, pairing tokens or secret-file contents.

Contracts and verdicts

The Companion displays provisional gameplay signals. The server creates the final result from final provider data. If evidence is incomplete, an internal review is opened automatically.

Contact

For Deadlock Contracts technical support or to report a security issue, message @deadlockts_bot on Telegram.

DATA SOURCES

Data sources

Steam

Steam confirms which account belongs to you. We may also retrieve its public name and avatar through the Steam API. Your Steam password is not shared with this site.

Deadlock match data

Final statistics may come from Deadlock API, an independent source run by the community. Results can arrive with a delay; we do not control this source's availability.

Companion / Overwolf GEP

The Companion receives supported events from your current game through the Overwolf Game Events Provider. They help track the match, but are provisional and cannot confirm contract completion on their own.

How contracts and XP are confirmed

After the match, the server checks final data against the signed contract conditions. Depending on the available verification method, it uses match-source data or eligible final Companion data. The server always decides completion and awarded XP; provisional progress in the app is not a reward. Leaderboard eligibility may follow different rules.

If data is still missing

Waiting or further review does not mean failure. Open the active case to see the verification state and the next step. If you need help, visit support.
